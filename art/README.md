# The Loft cast

A pigeon courier carries every payment, and each person picks one of nine
creatures: cheetah, fennec fox, lion, African grey parrot, pangolin, tortoise,
hornbill, honey badger or chameleon. The first four are featured on the welcome
screen; the picker scrolls through all nine. Generated with Higgsfield
(`nano_banana_pro`, 2k, 3:2, text only), backgrounds removed with Higgsfield's
background remover, then cut into one WebP per pose by `scripts/slice-cast.ts`
into `web/public/cast/`. `art/concepts/lineup-v1.jpg` is the concept lineup the
cast was chosen from.

The full-size sheets are kept out of git (large); `sheets/preview/` has small
copies for reference.

## Shared style

3D CGI character pose sheet, rendered like a frame from a modern animated
feature film (physically based materials), explicitly not a 2D illustration.
One original mascot per sheet and nothing else, six poses in two rows by three
columns with generous empty space, full body in every cell, small pose numbers
underneath. Chunky appealing proportions, individually rendered fur or feathers
with soft subsurface scattering, glossy eyes, bold saturated colours, dramatic
three-point studio lighting with a warm rim light, plain light warm-grey
background. The envelope is cream with a terracotta wax seal.

Don't pass the concept lineup as a reference image: the model copies its other
characters into the sheet as ghosts.

## What drives each pose (nothing is decorative)

- The courier flies from the sender to the recipient while the payment is in
  flight, circles until the relay reports the transaction confirmed, then lands.
  The flight lasts as long as the real confirmation.
- A link's courier lands on the recipient's roof and waits there (perch) until
  they open it; then it hands over (handoff) and their creature catches it.
- Your creature on Home cheers for a few seconds when your balance actually goes
  up, and looks worried only when someone keeps you topped up and your balance
  has fallen under a quarter of that target.
